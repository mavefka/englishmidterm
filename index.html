🗣️ Café Hub: Günlük Konuşma Kalıpları (Sınavda Diyalog Tamamlama)

U1: Tanışma & Selamlaşma (s. 8) • How's it going? / How are you doing? ➔ I'm good. How about you?
• What's new? (Ne var ne yok?) ➔ Not much. (Pek bir şey yok.)
• Long time, no see! (Uzun zamandır görüşemedik!)
U2: Tavsiye İsteme ve Verme (s. 18) • What should I do? / What do you suggest? (Ne yapmalıyım / Ne önerirsin?)
• You should / could + V1 (You should go and talk to them.)
• Why don't you / we + V1? (Why don't you do breathing exercises?)
• How about + V-ing? (How about using some earplugs? - ⚠️ -ing alır!)
U3: Tren İstasyonunda Bilgi Sorma (s. 28) • Could I have a ticket to...? ➔ Would you like a single (tek yön) or a return (gidiş-dönüş)?
• Do I have to change trains? (Aktarma yapmalı mıyım?)
• What platform does it leave from? (Hangi perondan kalkıyor?)
U4: Buluşma Ayarlama (s. 38) • Would you like to hang out tonight? (Bu akşam takılmak ister misin?)
• Are you free on Saturday? (Cumartesi müsait misin?)
• How about Sunday instead? (Onun yerine pazar nasıl olur?)
U5: İş Görüşmesi / Mülakat (s. 48-49) • Currently, I'm working for a law firm. (Şu anda bir hukuk firmasında çalışıyorum.)
• I'm good at working under pressure. (Baskı altında çalışmakta iyiyimdir.)
• I've learnt how to manage my time / deal with clients.
U6: Eczanede Semptom Anlatma (s. 58) • What are your symptoms? / Are you allergic to anything?
• Do you have anything for a sore throat? (Boğaz ağrısı için bir şeyiniz var mı?)
• My back hurts. / I have a terrible cold.

✍️ Sınavlık Writing & Edat (Preposition) Şifresi

📌 Sınavda Çıkabilecek Edat (Preposition) Eşleşmeleri:

• Adjective + Prep (U1, s.7): involved in | good at | responsible for | happy with | interested in | keen on

• Verb + Prep (U2, s.14): look for (aramak) | rely on (güvenmek) | help with (yardım etmek) | talk to someone about something | argue with someone about something

• Work + Prep (U5, s.47-50): work for a company (şirket için) | work in IT / marketing / a hospital (sektör/mekan) | work at a library / university

📝 Örnek Writing Modeli (50-60 Kelime Kendini ve Rutinini Tanıtma):

"Hi! My name is Umut and I am a university student in Yalova. Currently, I am living in a dormitory. I am keen on technology and music. Most of the time, I study English and computer engineering on weekdays. At the weekend, I usually hang out with my friends or go for a coffee. In the future, I hope to work for an international software company."

👉 Neden Tam Puan Alır? Çünkü içinde U1 (keen on, most of the time), U2 (Present Simple & Continuous), U3 (hope to work), U4 (hang out, go for a coffee) ve U5 (work for) kalıplarının hepsi var!

🚨 Sınavda Sakın Yapma (Top 5 Hata):

1. Everyone / Somebody gördüğünde çoğul fiil koyma! (Everyone is / has / uses)

2. Did / Didn't kullandığın cümlede fiili 2. halde bırakma! (I didn't think, didn't thought)

3. Since'ten sonra süreç koyma, başlangıç koy! (since 2020 / for 6 years)

4. Mustn't (yasak) ile Don't have to (zorunlu değil) farkını karıştırma!

5. Probably / Definitely kelimelerini will'den sonra, ama won't'tan önce yaz! (will probably go / probably won't go)
